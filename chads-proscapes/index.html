    How it comes together

    Consult, design, build, care.

    Most Proscapes jobs come from a neighbor’s recommendation. Here’s what happens after you make that call.

    01

    ConsultWalk the property together

    It starts with a free estimate. Call (817) 279-9085 and we’ll come look at the slope, the drainage, the sun and the way you want to use the space.

    What you get
    • Free, in-person estimate
    • Honest read on what the ground will allow
    • Se habla español
    02

    DesignYour ideas, made buildable

    We work with your ideas and help you through the design process, so the plan fits the house and the yard before a shovel hits the ground.

    What you get
    • Layout for beds, stone and lawn
    • Drainage and grading planned up front
    • Irrigation zones designed around the plants
    03

    BuildOne crew does the work

    Dirt work, stonework, beds, sod and sprinklers, all by the Proscapes crew. Irrigation is designed and installed under Texas Landscape Irrigator license LI0006268.

    What you get
    • Concrete & stone work, walls, walkways
    • Sod, hydromulch, trees and shrubs
    • Sprinkler or lake pump install
    04

    CareKeep it looking like day one

    Weekly mowing, hedging, mulching and fertilizing, plus sprinkler repairs, valve locates and controller programming when the seasons change.

    What you get
    • Weekly lawn maintenance
    • Sprinkler repair & diagnostics
    • Hardscape renewal
